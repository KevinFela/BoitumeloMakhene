Before your first run

A few useful details.

Which areas do you cover?

Send your collection and delivery suburbs with your request. Boitumelo will confirm whether she can assist before you book.

How much does a run cost?

Every errand is different. Share your list, locations and timing to receive a quote. The service fee and any shopping costs are agreed before the run.

Can I request a same-day errand?

You can ask for a same-day run. Availability and timing must be confirmed with Boitumelo; submitting an enquiry does not reserve a slot.

How do I pay?

Payment arrangements and any shopping budget are agreed directly with Boitumelo when she confirms your quote. This website does not collect payments.

Does filling in the form send my request?

The form prepares a message for you to review. Choose WhatsApp or email and send it there. Your booking is only confirmed once Boitumelo agrees the details with you.
